## 📌 About the Project
Space exploration doesn't end when a mission terminates. Across the Moon, Mars, and deep space, countless discarded spacecraft, rovers, landers, and scientific instruments rest as silent monuments of human curiosity and engineering triumph. 

**"Abandoned but not Forgotten"** is an interactive web-based virtual museum and educational platform designed to bridge the gap between historical space exploration and public awareness. We transform complex mission archives and scattered spatial data into engaging, accessible storytelling for students, educators, and space enthusiasts alike.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **🌍 Interactive Destination Explorer:** Seamlessly navigate through historic hardware left behind on the Moon, Mars, and deep space.
* **📜 Unified Artifact & Mission Archives:** Centralized logs detailing technical milestones, deployment dates, and the scientific legacy of iconic hardware.
* **🎨 Visual Storytelling:** Simplified, beginner-friendly narratives that replace dense scientific jargon with immersive exploration.
* **📱 Responsive & Modern UI:** Fully optimized, clean, and accessible web interface designed for learners of all ages.

---

## 🏛️ Featured Destinations & Hardware

### 1. 🌙 The Moon
* **Apollo Lunar Modules:** Descent stages (such as *Eagle*) left behind as historic launch platforms.
* **Lunar Roving Vehicles (LRVs):** Three electric moon buggies permanently parked on the lunar surface.
* **ALSEP Scientific Packages:** Seismometers, laser retroreflectors, and long-term research instruments.
* **Surveyor Landers:** 1960s robotic probes that proved soft lunar landings were possible.

### 2. 🔴 Mars & Deep Space
* **Mars Surface Hardware:** *Sojourner*, *Spirit*, *Opportunity*, and *Viking 1 & 2* landers/rovers studying ancient Martian terrain.
* **Ingenuity Helicopter:** The first powered aircraft on another world (retired after rotor damage).
* **Deep Space Pioneers:** *Pioneer 10 & 11* and *NEAR Shoemaker* (historic flybys and asteroid orbit insertion).
