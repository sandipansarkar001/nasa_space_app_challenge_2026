🌑 Lunar Regolith (Moon Dust)

Regolith is the layer of powdery gray dust and shattered rock covering the Moon, created by billions of years of meteorite crashes. Because there is no wind or rain on the Moon, astronaut footprints in the regolith will stay there for millions of years!
